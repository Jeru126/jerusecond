About Sports

Participating in sports fosters teamwork, endurance, and physical fitness. Staying active through regular athletics helps improve health while maintaining discipline and stress management for academic life. Beyond the physical benefits, the structured routine of training and competition instills time-management skills and sharpens focus, creating a healthy balance between rigorous studies and personal well-being.

Sports & Activities

  • Basketball: Improves agility, tactical thinking, and teamwork.
  • Running & Athletics: Builds endurance, mental resilience, and cardio health.
  • Football / Soccer: Enhances coordination, strategy, and endurance.
